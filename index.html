Meet the artist

Rooted in tradition. Made personal.

Henna Art by Sadia Amjid brings together traditional mehndi influences and contemporary body-art expression. Based in Dubai, the business offers henna experiences for bridal occasions, private appointments and celebrations.

From Arabic and Emirati-inspired designs to Indian, Pakistani, Moroccan and Sudanese influences, the aim is to help each client find a style that feels meaningful to them.
