# Henna Art by Sadia Amjid – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Henna Art by Sadia Amjid is a Dubai-based henna artistry business offering bridal mehndi, traditional and contemporary henna designs, personal appointments, event services, home visits, henna products and classes.

The redesigned homepage presents the business as a professional, personalised henna destination, targeting brides, individuals, families and event organisers. Its direction focuses on showcasing the range of henna services while making the business more trustworthy and enquiries easier to navigate.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Credibility and Trust Signals

- Removed placeholder team profiles and unsupported team information.
- Strengthened the introduction of Sadia Amjid and her henna artistry.
- Improved the presentation of business credentials and trust-related content without relying on conflicting client statistics.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website displayed inconsistent client figures, including 20,000+ and 25,000+, alongside repeated placeholder team profiles. These inconsistencies could undermine customer confidence.

### 2. Clearer Service Presentation and Navigation

- Organised the different henna offerings into clearer service categories.
- Gave bridal henna, personal appointments and event services more distinct presentation.
- Improved service discovery through relevant content and clearer enquiry pathways.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website promoted a wide range of services without making it easy for visitors to identify the most relevant option.

### 3. More Consistent Booking and Enquiry Experience

- Improved the visibility and consistency of booking and contact pathways.
- Structured the enquiry journey around relevant booking details, including event type, date, location, guest count and preferred henna style.
- Made the next steps in the enquiry process clearer.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website had fragmented enquiry forms and did not clearly communicate important booking information, making it harder for customers to understand how to proceed.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Strengthened the presentation of henna artistry and the business's traditional and contemporary design offerings.
- **Layout:** Improved the organisation of the homepage, service categories and key business information.
- **User Experience:** Made it easier for visitors to explore services and find relevant enquiry options.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of business identity, credibility information and booking pathways.
- **Content Structure:** Reduced ambiguity around service discovery and prioritised information relevant to potential customers.

## Overall Result

The redesigned homepage gives Henna Art by Sadia Amjid a clearer and more professional online presence. It addresses the three main website issues by improving credibility, organising the service offerings and creating a more consistent enquiry experience for customers interested in bridal, personal and event henna services.
